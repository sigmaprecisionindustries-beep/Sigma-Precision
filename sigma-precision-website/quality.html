Quality Assurance & Safety

Quality control process

  • First Article Inspection (FAI) before production starts
  • In-process dimensional checks at defined intervals
  • Tool offset verification for critical dimensions
  • Thread gauging (Go/No-Go) on all tapped holes
  • Surface finish measurement on finishing operations
  • Final inspection before dispatch

Safety protocols

  • Full machine guard enclosures
  • Three-tier signal lamps
  • Emergency stops at multiple positions
  • Mandatory PPE: glasses, steel-toe shoes, cut-resistant gloves
  • Fire extinguisher within 3 m of machines
